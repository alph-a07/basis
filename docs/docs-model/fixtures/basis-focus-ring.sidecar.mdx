---
id: basis-focus-ring
guidelines:
  do:
    - Pass the corner shape matching the underlying composable container.
    - Rely on `Color.Unspecified` to inherit the guaranteed 3.0:1 contrast focus color.
  dont:
    - Do not hide or suppress focus rings on keyboard-focusable elements.
    - Do not position elements with less than 2dp margins if focus rings might be clipped.
  notes:
    - Automatically conforms to `focus-visible` conventions, appearing only on keyboard navigation.
---

## Accessibility

`basisFocusRing` satisfies WCAG 2.4.7 (Focus Visible) and WCAG 2.4.11 (Focus Appearance) by
drawing a 2dp stroke with a 2dp outward offset, guaranteeing a minimum 3.0:1 contrast ratio
against both light and dark adjacent surfaces.
