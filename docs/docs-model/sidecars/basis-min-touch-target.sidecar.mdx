---
id: basis-min-touch-target
guidelines:
  do:
    - Apply to small action buttons, icon triggers, and compact chips (e.g. 24dp or 32dp visual size).
    - Allow ambient theme `ConstraintsControl.minTouchTarget` to govern the default 48dp floor.
  dont:
    - Do not hardcode dimensions smaller than 48dp unless targeting specialized embedded devices.
    - Do not position adjacent small targets so closely that their 48dp hitboxes overlap.
  notes:
    - Expands the interactive hit area without increasing visible padding or altering layout flow.
---

## Accessibility

`basisMinTouchTarget` fulfills WCAG 2.5.5 (Target Size) and Android Accessibility Scanner
requirements by expanding the hit test target of any component to at least 48dp × 48dp.
The layout bounds and visual drawing remain untouched, preventing clipping or unwanted gaps.
