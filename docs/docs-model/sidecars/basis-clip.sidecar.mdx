---
id: basis-clip
guidelines:
  do:
    - Use `basisClip` to constrain media, imagery, or overflowing children to shapes derived from `BasisTheme.radius` tokens.
    - Pair with `BasisTheme.radius` tokens to maintain visual consistency across containers.
  dont:
    - Do not clip containers whose children render focus rings or badges outside layout bounds.
    - Do not apply duplicate clipping when `basisSurface` already clips by default.
  notes:
    - Clipping is applied during the draw phase and does not alter measured layout dimensions.
---

## Accessibility

`basisClip` visually clips content to the specified shape boundary. For accessibility services
and TalkBack touch exploration, the semantic touch bounds reflect the layout dimensions.
