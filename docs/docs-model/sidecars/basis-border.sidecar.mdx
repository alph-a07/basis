---
id: basis-border
guidelines:
  do:
    - Pair with `BasisTheme.colors.border.muted` or `BasisTheme.colors.border.default` for subtle container boundaries.
    - Match the element corner shape to prevent visual anti-aliasing artifacts.
  dont:
    - Do not use high-contrast primary borders for non-interactive content separators.
    - Do not apply thick border strokes that reduce the effective content layout area.
  notes:
    - Hairline 1dp borders adapt automatically across light and dark mode surface steps.
---

## Accessibility

`basisBorder` renders decorative strokes conforming to the element's corner shape.
When used on interactive elements, ensure the adjacent container fill and border achieve
sufficient contrast against the surrounding background.
