## Overview

The `Surface` atom serves as the visual bedrock for components, mapping semantic elevations to background tones and border hairlines.

## Tonal Elevation

Unlike traditional drop-shadow elevation, Basis surfaces utilize tonal elevation in dark mode to preserve contrast and luminous depth.

## Borders & Corners

Surfaces accept token-driven border widths and corner radii conforming to the Basis spacing scale.
