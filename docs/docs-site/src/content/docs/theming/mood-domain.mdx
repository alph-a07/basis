Add `MoodIntent` and `DomainIntent` to your `BasisIntent` to control corner radii, spacing density, contrast, and motion — without adjusting individual tokens. See the full preset, knob, and domain tables in the [Intent reference](/docs/theming/intent-reference).

## 2. Decision Guide

**Start with a preset.** Choose one of the 7 archetypes on `MoodPreset` — for example `Professional` for information-dense products, `Playful` for rounded consumer apps, or `Neutral` (the default) for a balanced baseline. Full list: [MoodPreset](/docs/theming/intent-reference#moodpreset).

**Touch knobs only to deviate.** Use `MoodKnobs` when you need to fine-tune a single axis away from the preset baseline — for example `density = 0.2f` for dense data tables, or `roundness = -0.3f` for sharper edges. Full list: [MoodKnobs](/docs/theming/intent-reference#moodknobs).

**Select product domains.** `DomainIntent.domains` accepts a `Set<ProductDomain>`. Multiple domains can be combined; they are blended by conservative rules. If omitted, `domains` defaults to `emptySet()` (neutral baseline without industry biases). Full list: [ProductDomain](/docs/theming/intent-reference#productdomain).
