Basis models all theme colors in the **OKLCH** color space (Lightness, Chroma, Hue). This ensures uniform perceived brightness across all hues, keeping text legible in both light and dark modes.

## The Flaw in Traditional HSL

In legacy design systems, themes are often calculated using standard HSL. However, the human visual system is significantly more sensitive to green and yellow wavelengths than to blue wavelengths:

| Color | Nominal HSL Value | Perceived Lightness (L in OKLCH) | White Text Contrast |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pure Yellow** | `hsl(60, 100%, 50%)` | **0.97** (Nearly White) | 1.1:1 *(Illegible)* |
| **Pure Blue** | `hsl(240, 100%, 50%)` | **0.45** (Dark Navy) | 8.5:1 *(Legible)* |

Because HSL models both colors as having "50% lightness", automated systems relying on HSL often place unreadable white text on bright yellow buttons.

## Why Yellow Brands Still Get Readable Text

OKLCH models lightness according to human eye physiology:

- **`L` (Lightness)**: Directly reflects human brightness perception. A color at `L = 0.70` appears equally bright whether it is yellow, green, or blue.
- **`C` (Chroma)**: Color saturation, starting from neutral gray at `0.0`.
- **`H` (Hue)**: Angular position on the color wheel (0° to 360°).

Basis handles contrast through two mechanisms:

1. **Surface fills with paired content**: When a high-lightness brand color (such as yellow, `L ≈ 0.90+`) is used as a fill, Basis detects that white text fails the contrast floor and pairs the surface with dark content text (`L ≤ 0.20`), satisfying the ≥ 4.5:1 requirement.
2. **Foreground text and icons**: When brand-colored text or icons sit on standard surface backgrounds, Basis adjusts lightness (raising or lowering `L`) until the contrast floor is satisfied, while keeping the hue unchanged.

## Gamut Mapping and Clipping

Some vivid OKLCH coordinates fall outside standard sRGB display limits. Rather than allowing colors to clip arbitrarily — which causes unpredictable hue shifts — Basis applies gamut mapping that preserves hue angle and lightness while scaling back chroma to fit safely within sRGB boundaries.
